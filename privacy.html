Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 05, 2026 • Published by SD Soft

Welcome to SAKO ("we," "our," or "the Platform"). SAKO is an architectural real estate marketplace and property discovery application operated by SD Soft. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal data of our users across all supported platforms, including iOS, Android, and web interfaces.

Privacy Commitment & Global Standards

We respect your privacy as a fundamental right. SAKO adheres strictly to international data protection principles, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the developer policies of major application marketplaces. We never sell, rent, or monetize your personal information with third-party brokers or advertisers. You retain full control over your data, including the right to access, export, or permanently delete your account at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the necessary information required to provide reliable property discovery, tour scheduling, and account security:

A. Information You Provide Voluntarily

  • Account Credentials: When you register an account, we collect your full name, username, email address, and a securely hashed password. If you choose Single Sign-On (Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In), we receive standard tokenized identity confirmations.
  • Profile Details: Optional contact telephone number, profile photograph, and agency licensing credentials (for registered real estate offices and agents).
  • Listing Content & Media: Property photographs, architectural video walkthroughs (Reels), floor plan blueprints, pricing information, and listing descriptions that you choose to publish on the platform.
  • Inquiries & Tour Bookings: Communications between prospective buyers/renters and property representatives, appointment schedule requests, and customer support messages.

B. Information Collected Automatically or With Explicit Permission

  • Geolocation Coordinates (Optional): With your affirmative permission via operating system dialogs, we collect location coordinates solely to present nearby properties on interactive maps and compute distance estimates. You can deny or revoke location access at any time and still browse listings manually.
  • Device & Notification Tokens: Device model, operating system version (iOS or Android), and push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging) used strictly to deliver appointment updates, tour confirmations, and chat alerts.
  • Technical Logs: Server diagnostic logs, crash reports, and system telemetry used solely to maintain platform stability and troubleshoot connectivity issues.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is processed for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To authenticate user sessions, maintain account security, and prevent fraudulent sign-ups.
  • To enable property discovery, search filtering, and interactive mapping.
  • To facilitate direct communication and property tour scheduling between clients and verified agencies.
  • To deliver requested push notifications regarding appointment confirmations and listing updates.
  • To maintain the safety and integrity of the marketplace by enforcing our Terms of Service and reviewing reported content.

3. Third-Party Service Providers & Infrastructure

We partner with industry-leading infrastructure providers under strict confidentiality and security terms:

  • Authentication Services: Apple Sign-In and Google Identity services for secure, privacy-preserving authentication.
  • Cloud Infrastructure & Notifications: Google Cloud and Firebase for database operations, secure cloud asset storage, and push notification routing.
  • Mapping Services: Google Maps API for interactive property pin display and geographic search queries.

Zero Third-Party Advertising: SAKO does not integrate third-party ad networks, tracking pixels, or cross-app behavioural trackers. Your personal data is never shared with external marketing entities.

4. Account Deletion & Data Subject Rights

Every SAKO user has the right to access, rectify, or permanently delete their personal data. We provide direct and unconditional self-service account deletion:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the SAKO app → go to Profile → tap Settings (Gear Icon) → tap Delete Account → Confirm deletion.
  • Direct Support Deletion: You may also request account deletion at any time by emailing sdsoftteach@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

Upon confirming deletion, your user profile, saved favorites, contact records, and active listings are permanently purged from our active databases within 24 hours.

5. Data Security & Encryption

We employ robust technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information:

  • All data in transit between your mobile device and our servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • User passwords are encrypted using salted cryptographic hashing functions (bcrypt/Argon2); passwords are never stored in plaintext.
  • Access to backend databases and infrastructure is strictly limited to authorized engineering personnel using role-based access controls (RBAC) and multi-factor authentication.

6. Children's Privacy

SAKO is not directed to individuals under the age of 13 (or under the minimum legal age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that personal information of a child has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we take immediate steps to delete such data.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect enhancements to our services or changes in legal regulations. When material modifications occur, the updated policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
