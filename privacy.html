Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 05, 2026 • Published by SD Soft

Welcome to Sako ("we," "our," or "us"). We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled securely, responsibly, and transparently. This Privacy Policy explains how our iOS application, web services, and cloud platform collect, utilize, and safeguard your information.

Apple App Store Compliance & Guarantee

Sako strictly respects Apple’s Privacy Guidelines. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. You retain full control over your account and may permanently delete your data at any time directly within the application.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Sako application, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, username, and encrypted password when registering, or verified profile identity tokens if signing in with Apple ID or Google.
  • Profile Details: Optional phone number, profile photo, and agency affiliations (for licensed real estate representatives).
  • Property Content: Photographs, architectural video walkthroughs (Reels), floor plan blueprints, and property descriptions that you voluntarily publish.
  • Location Coordinates (Optional): With your explicit consent via iOS system dialogs, we collect GPS coordinates solely to showcase nearby property listings on interactive map views.
  • Device & Notification Identifiers: Device type and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) push tokens used strictly to deliver appointment tour updates and messaging notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is utilized exclusively for genuine real estate services:

  • Authenticating user sessions and providing account security.
  • Facilitating property tour scheduling between clients and verified agencies.
  • Displaying localized property listings based on city or proximity filters.
  • Preventing malicious fraud, spam postings, and account impersonation.

3. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

We work with trusted industry providers for infrastructure:

  • Apple Sign-In: For privacy-preserving biometric and single-tap authentication.
  • Google Firebase: For encrypted user authentication and push notification delivery.
  • Google Cloud Maps API: For rendering property pins and street map views.

We do NOT integrate third-party ad trackers, data brokers, or behavioral analytics SDKs that track you across other apps or websites.

4. Account Deletion & Data Retention

In accordance with Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v), Sako provides an immediate, user-initiated account deletion mechanism:

  • In-App Deletion: Open Sako → Profile → Settings (Gear Icon) → Tap "Delete Account" → Confirm.
  • Email Request: Send an email from your registered email address to sdsoftteach@gmail.com with subject "Delete My Account".

Upon deletion, your personal credentials, contact history, saved listings, and uploaded reels are permanently purged from our database within 24 hours.

5. Data Security

All network communications between the Sako mobile application and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Sensitive database records are protected with industry-standard cryptographic hashing (bcrypt/Argon2).

6. Children’s Privacy

Sako does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that personal data of a minor has been collected without parental consent, we immediately delete the record.
