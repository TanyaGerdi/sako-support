User Account Management & Data Rights

We respect your privacy and ownership over your personal data. In compliance with international data protection standards (including GDPR and mobile platform security policies across iOS and Android), users have the right to request access to, export, or permanently delete their account and associated records at any time. You can delete your account directly inside the app by going to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing our data protection officer at sdsoftteach@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". All personal credentials and listings are purged within 24 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account or sign in to SAKO?

You can create a free account using your name, email address, and a secure password, or utilize convenient Single Sign-On (SSO) options including Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In. All authentication workflows are protected by industry-standard TLS encryption.

How do I schedule a property tour?

When viewing any verified property listing in the app, tap the "Schedule Tour" button to select your preferred date and time slot. The property's representative or licensed agency receives the request instantly and confirms your appointment via in-app notification.

What are SAKO Architectural Reels?

SAKO Reels provide an engaging, high-definition video walkthrough format (up to 60 seconds) allowing property owners and verified agencies to showcase architecture, interior finishes, and panoramic views directly to prospective buyers and tenants.

How are subscriptions and premium features billed?

Real estate agency subscriptions and listing boost packages are billed securely through the native in-app billing systems of your device platform (Apple In-App Purchases on iOS / Google Play Billing on Android). You can view, modify, or cancel your active subscription at any time through your device's subscription settings.

Why does the app request Location and Media permissions?

Device permissions are optional and requested only to provide core app features. Location access enables the interactive map view and helps calculate distance to properties and local amenities. Photo and camera access is requested only if you choose to upload property photos, video reels, or an account avatar. Permissions can be updated at any time in your device settings.
