Account Management & Data Deletion Rights

Per Apple Safety and Privacy Guidelines, all users have the right to delete their account and all associated data at any time. You can delete your account directly inside the Sako app by going to Profile → Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing our data protection officer at sdsoftteach@gmail.com with subject "Delete Account Request". All personal records and property listings are purged within 24 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account or sign in to Sako?

You can create a free account using your name, email address, and password, or use one-tap "Sign in with Apple" or Google Sign-In. All authentication is protected with industry-standard TLS encryption.

How do I schedule a physical or virtual property tour?

Browse any curated property in the app, tap "Schedule Tour", and select your desired date and time slot. The verified real estate agency or representative will confirm the appointment directly in your app notifications.

What are Sako Architectural Reels?

Sako Reels allows verified real estate offices and property owners to publish high-definition video walkthroughs (up to 60 seconds) showcasing interior architecture, lighting, and panoramic property views.

How do subscriptions and Apple In-App Purchases work?

Real estate office subscriptions and premium features are billed securely through your Apple ID via the App Store. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your iPhone/iPad Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Why does Sako request Location permissions?

Location permission is completely optional. When enabled, Sako displays nearby residences on an interactive map and calculates distances to key city landmarks. You can decline location access and still search properties manually.
